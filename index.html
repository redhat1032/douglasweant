T-05 · BRG 247 · Outside the Build

The interests that keep the systems view human.

These are not credentials or a second brand. They are the things that keep me curious about how people, institutions, and technology behave when the plan meets reality.

Post-Apocalyptic Fiction

I read the genre to examine what systems, social structures, and values endure when infrastructure fails. The best stories are rarely about collapse alone; they are about adaptation, trust, and rebuilding.

Gaming

World of Warcraft is a long-running hobby and a useful reminder that complex systems depend on coordination, shared context, leadership, and recovery after a bad decision.

Coffee

A simple constant for long-form technical work, reading, and the kind of conversations that need time rather than speed.

Food History

I research pre-contact South American food systems and the Columbian Exchange: how ingredients, trade, knowledge, and culture moved across the world. It is a living study in adaptation, interdependence, and unintended consequences.
