The Core Thesis

Behavior over Bytes.

Technical controls catch events; behavioral insight catches intent. My work bridges the gap between digital forensics and cyberpsychology, understanding not just what happened on a network, but the psychological escalation that led to the event.

The Perspective

The Cyber-Physical Seam.

In industrial manufacturing, every sensor and control system is a network endpoint. I believe the future of security lies in this seam, where physical environments and digital worlds intersect, and where behavioral precursors often signal risk long before a breach occurs.

The Mission

WeanTech LLC.

WeanTech is an independent research and consulting practice focused on behavioral security, insider threat detection, and the cyber-physical seam. I build systems that acknowledge human nature rather than fighting it.
