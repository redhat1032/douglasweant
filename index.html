03 / About

A few things about me.

I'm Douglas Weant. My background includes food-manufacturing R&D, where real-world constraints have a way of testing every elegant theory.

Earlier chapters included service as a Navy Signalman and Master-at-Arms, and in Air Force Security Forces - CATM. I've since completed undergraduate and graduate study in cybersecurity and am finishing graduate work in computer science.

Those experiences left me interested in how technology, people, and decisions interact—especially when things don't go according to plan.

Outside of work, my curiosity wanders. Post-apocalyptic fiction, gaming, coffee, and the history of food all have a way of leading back to questions about adaptation, cooperation, and the systems we depend on.

This website isn't a pitch deck. It's a place to collect the things I'm learning and the questions I haven't answered yet.
