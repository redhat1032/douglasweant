The Operating Question

What must still work when ordinary infrastructure cannot be assumed?

I am interested in the capabilities communities and organizations need when networks are down, cloud services are unreachable, or the available information is incomplete. The work is practical: preserve communication, situational awareness, and accountable decision-making when conditions become difficult.

The Perspective

At the cyber-physical edge.

My experience in security, law enforcement, and regulated manufacturing informs a systems view: digital failures have physical consequences, and technology is only useful if people can operate it under pressure. This is where security, continuity, communications, and human factors converge.

The Laboratory

WeanTech LLC.

I founded WeanTech as an applied R&D company for resilient systems. Its first research program, Project MESH, asks whether a small team can establish secure local communications in 15 minutes or less without commercial Internet or cellular service. AI is a tool in that work — not the product, the premise, or the only path to a working capability. A second line of inquiry, Project ARENA, examines autonomous agents operating under adversarial conditions.
