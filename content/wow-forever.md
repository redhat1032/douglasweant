# All Things WoW Forever

A home base for Doug’s time in *World of Warcraft: Forever* — his Dwarf Rogue, the PvP focus, the professions, and a running list of official news.

## Dwarf Rogue

No character name has been decided. This page refers to him as Doug’s Dwarf Rogue.

Doug’s character in *WoW: Forever* is a Dwarf Rogue on the Alliance side, focused on PvP.

Dwarf Rogue is the rarest race and class combination in WoW Forever, at about 0.3% according to fan censuses. That figure is from fan censuses, not official Blizzard data.

## World PvP and duels

Rogue is rated top for world PvP and duels: stealth openers, Cheap Shot, Kidney Shot, Blind, Vanish.

Dwarf Stoneform is one of the top defensive racials.

## Engineering and Mining

Professions: Engineering and Mining, chosen for PvP.

## Patch notes and news

Forever is only a couple of weeks old, so official hotfixes are still landing often. This space is a short briefing, not a complete patch log: a few current headlines, each in plain language, with a link to the source. Dates below follow the official posts.

- *World of Warcraft: Forever* is live worldwide, a standing level 1–60 version of Azeroth that sits alongside modern and Classic WoW. [Blizzard, “Now Live”](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-goes-live-globally-november-4)
- The first raids open on December 9, 2026: the 10-player Barrow Deeps, the 20-player Hyjal Summit, and the 40-player Onyxia’s Lair. [Blizzard raid overview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303009/world-of-warcraft-forever-raid-overviewlocations-raid-sizes-and-attunement)
- The same November 13 notes say dungeon kills grant no experience to anyone in the party if the enemy is already trivial to one member. [Blizzard hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-13-2026)
