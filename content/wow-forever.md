# All Things WoW Forever

A home base for Doug’s time in *World of Warcraft: Forever* — the character, the leveling plan, the professions, and a running list of official news.

## Syn Ward

Syn Ward is a Dwarf Priest on the Alliance side of *WoW: Forever*, playing on the US mega server (no realms; characters use two-part names now). He was created on November 16, 2026, and he started in Dun Morogh, where his first quest was curbing the wolf overrun in Coldridge Valley and bringing back eight pieces of meat — he returned victorious.

Dwarf Priest is the rarest Priest pairing on US Normal: about 0.3% of tracked characters, roughly 5,000 of some 1.32 million. He is not a novelty pick. He is the Forever rebirth of Doug’s classic-era Priest: the one who, auditioning for a guild in Lower Blackrock Spire, kept an accidental full-room pull at zero deaths and got the invite on the spot; and who in Upper Blackrock Spire was the last one standing in a near-wipe and finished the boss alone — “Did that priest just melt that boss’s face?”

Doug is a lifelong Alliance player, so this is also the home faction. The name: Syn keeps him in Doug’s SYN/ACK naming world; Ward is the Priest’s promise — Fear Ward, and the ward still standing when the pull goes wrong. He levels as Shadow, with a healing second spec planned at 40 through Dual Specialization. The armory won’t list him until he logs out at level 10 or higher, so this page will get more precise as he grows.

## Leveling and Shadow

This is a condensed Shadow leveling guide for an Alliance Dwarf Priest in *WoW: Forever*. Talent spreads are still settling, and raids open on December 9, so treat this as a working plan.

### Talents

Early points go into Wand Specialization and Spirit Tap, then down the Shadow tree toward Shadowform at 40. Vampiric Embrace and Shadowform cut downtime — that is why guides recommend Shadow for Priest leveling. At 40, Dual Specialization adds a healing spec for dungeons without giving up Shadow for questing.

### Rotation

The wand is the leveling Priest’s real weapon until Shadowform — it costs no mana and carries your damage. The working pattern: open with your Shadow spells, with Shadow Word: Pain up and Mind Blast, then wand the target down so Spirit Tap and mana regen do their work. Heal between pulls, not during wanding, when you can avoid it. After Shadowform at 40, the rotation becomes spell-led.

### Stat priority

Wand DPS comes first — a better wand is the biggest leveling upgrade a young Priest can make. After that, Spirit, which fuels Spirit Tap and regen, then Intellect, then Shadow damage and spell power as gear offers it.

### Leveling route

Start in Dun Morogh, then Loch Modan and Westfall in the teens. Redridge, Duskwood, and the Wetlands carry the 20s. Take the classic Alliance road through the 30s, with Forever’s new mid-level zone, the Riverglades (about 35 to 45, reached overland from Redridge), as the main stop. Then the usual late path: Tanaris, Feralas, the Hinterlands, Un’Goro, the Plaguelands, and Winterspring, ending at Mount Hyjal at 60.

Quest more than you grind, and group with people near your level. A November 13 hotfix grants no dungeon kill experience to anyone in the party if the enemy is already trivial to one member, so a much higher-level friend can quietly wipe the XP from a run.

### Key milestones

**Level 5/13.** The Enchanting wands arrive — the Lesser Magic Wand, usable at level 5, and the Greater Magic Wand at level 13 (see Tailoring and Enchanting, below).

**Level 20.** Fear Ward: in Forever every Priest learns it at 20, so it is no longer a Dwarf exclusive — but a Dwarf Priest still wears it best.

**Level 40.** Shadowform, and Dual Specialization unlocks the healing second spec.

**Level 60.** Mount Hyjal is the level-60 zone, and the first raids open on December 9.

## Tailoring and Enchanting

Tailoring and Enchanting are the self-sufficiency pair, chosen because Syn Ward stands alone: he makes his own cloth armor, his own bags, and his own wands, and he disenchants his own replaced gear to keep Enchanting fed. Tailoring turns the cloth he loots anyway into armor and bags, and its unwanted greens become Enchanting materials. Late-game Tailoring adds Shadow cloth gear for a Shadow Priest.

Enchanting is the wand profession, and the wand is a leveling Priest’s main weapon. In Forever, Enchanting starts with a practice wand at skill 1; the Lesser Magic Wand (usable at level 5) and the Greater Magic Wand (level 13, roughly 50% more DPS) are crafter-made. Keep the wand current before almost any other gear.

There are no live Auction House prices in this section, because a reliable public price feed was not available when it was written. Check the US mega-server AH before you craft a large batch of anything.
