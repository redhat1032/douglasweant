Academic Path · Behavioral Interests

Investigating the human factor.

My research focus is shifting from purely technical detection toward the behavioral precursors of cyber-risk and insider threats.

Current Academic Focus

MS in Computer Science (AI/ML)

I am in the final stages of my Master’s at WGU. My work focuses on how machine learning can be applied to behavioral anomaly detection—identifying shifts in system usage that suggest a human element is deviating from the norm.

Future Direction

Psychology and Insider Threat

I am deeply interested in the psychological escalation patterns that precede security incidents. Rather than just catching a breach after it happens, I am exploring how organizational strain and behavioral drift can be identified earlier in the timeline.

To ground this, I am considering a return to WGU for a BS in Psychology to provide a formal framework for my interests in forensic cyberpsychology.

Research Themes

Behavioral Drift

Identifying the gap between authorized access and actual operational behavior.

Adversarial Psychology

Understanding the decision-making process of an attacker to build better defensive friction.
