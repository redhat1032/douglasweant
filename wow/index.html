Ack Syn

Ack Syn is a Forsaken Paladin on the Horde side of WoW: Forever, playing on the US mega server (no realms; characters use two-part names now). He’s currently around level 6 or 7 and leveling as Retribution, which is the usual solo path for Paladins in this game.

Forsaken Paladins are new in Forever, and their story sits close to home. After the starting graveyard in Deathknell and the usual work around Brill, the class is pulled west to Bandarion Keep in the Whispering Forest of Tirisfal Glades. That’s where the Light-in-undeath story lives, and it’s where most of Ack Syn’s class quests begin.

The long-term prize is a Forsaken-only epic mount at 60, earned through the Justice and Retribution quest chain. Closer to the ground, he’s picking up Mining and Blacksmithing so the ore he sees on the road can become plate, weapons, and a little gold along the way. The armory won’t list him until he logs out at level 10 or higher, so this page will get more precise as he grows.

Leveling and Retribution

This is a condensed Retribution guide for a Horde Forsaken Paladin in WoW: Forever. Talent spreads and gear advice are still settling, and raids do not open until December 9, so treat this as a working plan.

Talents

The current community leveling and endgame spread is 12 points in Holy, 8 in Protection, and 31 in Retribution. One published path, checked against live talent trees in mid-November, spends early points on cheaper instant abilities and critical strike, takes Seal of Command at 20, and reaches the capstone Unsealed Wrath at 40. After that, leftover points go into Strength, Seal damage, Intellect, and hit.

Unsealed Wrath is Forever’s answer to old-school Seal twisting. Your swings store copies of the Seal you have up; a hit with a different Seal spends those copies. Vengeance, the damage buff that stacks after crits, needs Seal of Command in this version of the game. A Holy-heavy “Shockadin” build exists, but it cannot also take Unsealed Wrath in a single 51-point layout. If you later want to heal or tank, Dual Specialization at 40 is the clean way to keep Retribution for questing.

Respec at a class trainer in a major city, and read the in-game tooltips: the trees moved more than once just before launch.

Rotation

Until Seal of Command, keep Seal of Righteousness up and use whatever weapon has the highest damage per second. From 20 on, move to a slow two-hander and live in Seal of Command. On enemies that will last more than a few seconds, you can open with Seal of the Crusader, Judge it, then swap to Command.

From there the priority is simple: Judgement on cooldown (it no longer consumes your Seal), Holy Strike on cooldown, Hammer of Wrath when a target is low, and Exorcism against Undead or Demons. Once you have Unsealed Wrath, bank a couple of Command stacks, swap to Seal of Righteousness for one swing, then go back to Command. Consecration is for packs, not for a single target when mana is tight.

A mid-November hotfix matters here: most Judgements now count as ranged attacks, so they use ranged hit and no longer trigger melee procs. Judgement of Command is the exception and is treated as melee again. Paladins still have no interrupt and no slow, so Hammer of Justice is how you stop a dangerous cast or a runner.

Stat priority

Your weapon is the biggest upgrade you can make. After that, look for Hit, then a mix of Strength (or attack power) and Spell Power or Holy damage. Intellect is more useful than it used to be, because one of the later Retribution talents turns part of it into Spell Damage and because this spec is hungry for mana. Some current guides also list Expertise as a new Forever stat; that is worth confirming on live tooltips before you chase it. Exact hit caps may still shift as more level-60 gear appears.

Leveling route

Start in Deathknell, work Brill and eastern Tirisfal, and detour west to Bandarion Keep for the Paladin story. The zone’s only flight master is in the Undercity. Around level 10 to 12, head south into Silverpine Forest, fit in the new Ruins of Lordaeron dungeon beside the city, then take the zeppelin to Orgrimmar and join everyone else in the Barrens.

From the mid-20s, mix Hillsbrad, Stonetalon, Ashenvale, and Thousand Needles, and run Shadowfang Keep or Blackfathom Deeps when you can. The 30s are Desolace, Arathi, Alterac (including the new City of Dalaran dungeon), and Stranglethorn Vale, with Scarlet Monastery a short ride from the Undercity. Forever’s large new mid-level zone, the Riverglades, is the main stop from about 35 to 45 — nearly two hundred quests, reached by the Steamwheedle ship from Tanaris or overland from Redridge. After that, the usual Horde path still works: Feralas, Tanaris, the Hinterlands, Un’Goro, the Plaguelands, and Winterspring, ending at Mount Hyjal at 60.

Quest more than you grind, and group with people near your level. A November 13 hotfix grants no dungeon kill experience to anyone in the party if the enemy is already trivial to one member, so a much higher-level friend can quietly wipe the XP from a run.

Key milestones

Level 10. You get your first talent point, plus Seal of Fury and Righteous Fury. Holy Strike, the new instant attack, should already be trained from level 6.

Level 20. Consecration and Blessing of Kings are baseline for every Paladin. This is also when Seal of Command comes online if you follow the Retribution path above. Around 18 to the mid-20s, finish the class weapon chain that starts at Bandarion Keep; the reward, Wolfsbane, is a slow two-hand sword that fits Command well.

Level 40. You can wear plate, take a second talent setup, and learn the free warhorse from A Paladin’s Steed at Bandarion Keep. Unsealed Wrath unlocks here, and the rotation above starts in earnest.

Level 60. Danitha Morr at the keep starts the Justice and Retribution chain for the Forsaken Charger. It is a long shopping list and a group fight in Scholomance; some middle steps may still get clarified as more players finish it. After that, Mount Hyjal is the level-60 zone, and the first raids open on December 9.

Mining and Blacksmithing

Mining and Blacksmithing are the straightforward profession pair for a Paladin who wants to make plate and weapons while leveling. There are no live Auction House prices in this section, because a reliable public price feed was not available when it was written. Check the US mega-server AH before you craft a large batch of anything.

Ack Syn can start immediately. Mining has no character-level requirement, and Walter Mason trains Apprentice Mining upstairs in Deathknell. Buy a Mining Pick, turn on Find Minerals, and smelt as you go — smelting also raises Mining skill, and bars usually sell more readily than raw ore. Bandarion Keep has a Blacksmithing trainer as well, so you do not have to wait for Orgrimmar. Johan Focht in the Sepulcher and Brom Killian in the Undercity can take Mining all the way to Artisan. For Blacksmithing, Expert comes from Saru Steelfury in Orgrimmar once you have the skill and are at least level 20; Artisan is trained by Brikk Keencraft in Stranglethorn Vale.

Mine what the zone already wants you to quest through. Copper in Tirisfal and Silverpine, tin in the Barrens and the mid-20s zones, iron in Thousand Needles, Desolace, Stranglethorn, or Arathi, mithril in the Badlands, Tanaris, and Searing Gorge, then thorium in the Burning Steppes and the Plaguelands. Train the next Mining rank at 50, 125, and 200 so the cap stays ahead of you.

What tends to be in demand, relatively speaking: mid-tier bars for other players leveling the same crafts (Forever reportedly makes some Blacksmithing recipes turn grey sooner, so those crafters burn more metal), stone and gems that come with the veins, and thorium or Arcanite once people are gearing for raids. Forever also adds scarce extras — Pyrite from early veins, Bauxite from iron and mithril, and Pitchblende from thorium, which smelts with coal into Azerothium at high Mining skill. Legionite, used in some new plate, is an Alchemy transmute with a long cooldown, not something a miner smelts directly.

The late Blacksmithing skill-up path published in community guides ends on a new dagger, Bold Dirk, that asks for a lot of thorium, Azerothium, and Essence of Air. Look at the Auction House before you commit those materials; if the listing is not worth the ingredients, stop on an earlier thorium recipe and keep the bars. Bind-on-Pickup pieces, including some of the nicer Paladin crafts, cannot go on the AH at all. Bind-on-Equip helms and legs that Retribution players are already talking about are the clearer “craft to sell or wear” targets, once you can confirm they have buyers.

Two Forever-only habits help on the road: sit at a camp long enough to use profession camp features (Mining’s Lodestone will not stack with Blessing of Might), and turn in waylaid ore crates for Merchant’s Favor when the AH is already flooded with the same bar.
