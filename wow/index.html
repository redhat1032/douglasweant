Patch notes and news

Forever is only a couple of weeks old, so official hotfixes are still landing often. This space is a short briefing, not a complete patch log: a few current headlines, each in plain language, with a link to the source. Dates below follow the official posts.

  • World of Warcraft: Forever is live worldwide, a standing level 1–60 version of Azeroth that sits alongside modern and Classic WoW. Blizzard, “Now Live”

  • The first raids open on December 9, 2026: the 10-player Barrow Deeps, the 20-player Hyjal Summit, and the 40-player Onyxia’s Lair. Blizzard raid overview

  • The same November 13 notes say dungeon kills grant no experience to anyone in the party if the enemy is already trivial to one member. Blizzard hotfixes
