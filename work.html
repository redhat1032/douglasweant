Current Build

Project MESH

Through WeanTech, I am developing a small research demonstrator for secure, infrastructure-independent local communications. The aim is to test a practical field question: can a small team deploy a useful communications capability quickly, without relying on commercial Internet, cellular service, or cloud infrastructure?

Working Standard

Evidence before claims.

I use a simple pattern: define the operating conditions, build the smallest honest prototype, test the limits, and document what the results support. AI is considered only where it improves a specific task while remaining bounded, auditable, and replaceable by a non-AI fallback.
